# SozoRock publication standard

Approved reference: SozoRock Outlook: Access, Capacity and Trust, October 2026, the nine-page edition approved by Dr. Oluwabiyi Adeyemo. The series name is SozoRock Outlook; each edition is titled "SozoRock Outlook: Access, Capacity and Trust, [Month Year]" unless he renames it. Do not reuse another publisher's series title. Use this as the standing template for subsequent outlooks unless he requests a change. Do not restart the design or ask him to repeat these preferences.

## Editorial approach

National scope across health access, workforce capacity, and AI and society. Local examples, including New York State, may support the analysis without limiting its scope. Write an original, concise assessment: a finding, the explanation, the decision it informs, and the result to monitor. Use precise institutional language. Avoid generic consulting language, internal workflow terms, decorative claims, and unsupported certainty.

The working package is a public article of about 500–650 words, a nine-page downloadable report, and platform-specific social posts. The approved report has 2,495 extracted words including labels, source notes, and footers. Preserve its density and proportion rather than adding filler to meet an exact count.

McKinsey is an editorial and design benchmark. The approved SozoRock edition is the actual production template. Keep original wording, charts, cover artwork, brand, and licensed fonts. Do not imply McKinsey authorship, approval, endorsement, or affiliation.

## Report structure

1. Cover: Foundation wordmark, economic research label, the series label "SozoRock Outlook", the title "Access, Capacity and Trust", the edition month in blue serif below it, a short deck, author, and the original geometric cover.
2. Opening assessment: main argument, one material finding, and a short explanation of the evidence base.
3. Six analytical pages: one finding per page, supporting prose, one numbered exhibit, units and source notes, and implications.
4. Final page: the next six to twelve months, institutional choices, conditions to monitor, methods, author, ORCID, and suggested citation.

Each chart states a finding in its headline. Identify units, population, period, and denominators. Keep annual rates, annualized quarterly rates, percentages, and percentage points distinct. State limitations only where they change interpretation. Do not treat adoption as effectiveness, expectations as forecasts, correlations as causes, or separate survey responses as mutually exclusive groups. Use authoritative primary sources and check the exact release vintage before publication. Do not describe secondary analysis as an original survey.

## Locked visual specifications

- US Letter, 612 × 792 points; nine pages.
- Main text starts at x = 139 points, width = 410 points, right edge = 549 points.
- Source Serif 4 for the wordmark, cover title, analytical headings, and major callouts. Instrument Sans for text, chart labels, and notes. Supplied licensed font files are authoritative.
- Cover title: 38 pt with 41 pt leading. Cover deck: 13.4 pt with 18 pt leading. Cover byline: 10 pt.
- Main text: 10 pt with 13.5 pt leading. Analytical headings: 13.3 pt with 16 pt leading. Interpretation paragraphs: 9.8 pt with 13.2 pt leading.
- Exhibit headline: 11.7 pt with 14.5 pt leading. Metric line: 8.6 pt with 11.2 pt leading. Source notes: 7.45 pt with 9.7 pt leading. Never shrink body copy to solve a layout problem.
- Palette: navy #071D3B, cobalt #0644AD, royal #0B2F6E, light blue #A9C6F0, pale blue #DCEAFF, ice #EFF5FF, supporting text #33495D, and white.
- Footer title and page number: 7.3 pt, top coordinate 759 points. Keep content clear of the footer; analytical content must end above 730 points.
- Close every exhibit with the publisher mark "The SozoRock Foundation" in the wordmark serif, below its source notes. Prose figures that are not charted carry their own linked source line on the page.
- No divider lines or hairlines; separate blocks with space.
- Use vector text and charts, embedded fonts, clickable sources, and bookmarks. No page screenshots in the report. Inspect every page at normal reading size and magnified.

The implementation in build.py is the measurement reference. Preserve the visual system while updating the analysis and dates. The article may use the report serif for editorial headings while retaining the Foundation website navigation, brand colors, and controls.

## Publication and distribution

Give every edition one permanent Foundation article URL. Make the abstract and article publicly readable before the free PDF download. Use the established email-verification service for the PDF. Keep marketing consent separate and optional. Include readable citations and author attribution, a canonical URL, a search sitemap entry, and accurate social previews. Do not promise indexing or citations.

Link previews use a title card: series label, title, edition month, author, and the cover's geometric field. Posts open with the edition and then the findings, for example "The latest SozoRock Outlook is out. In this edition: ...", followed by the article link.

X: one sharp finding, its implication, and the article link. Keep the final post within 280 characters including the platform-shortened link. Use a legible chart graphic.

LinkedIn: develop the finding and institutional implication in a few short paragraphs. Link to the same article and attach the matching graphic. Keep author and Foundation versions distinct.

Instagram: use the 4:5 graphic and a concise caption with the source and a clear route to the website article. Do not claim that a caption URL is clickable or that a bio link has been changed when it has not.

Use the existing author and Foundation accounts. Do not create separate social accounts for an outlook. Record actual published links; distinguish prepared drafts from posts that are live.
